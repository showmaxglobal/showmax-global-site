Two markets, one bridge

The India–UAE CEPA, in force since May 2022, has slashed tariffs and opened market access across more than 100 sectors — and a Rupee–Dirham settlement system now lets businesses trade in local currencies. The doors between Indore and the Gulf have never been more open. We help you walk through them with a plan, not a guess.
