Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At Showmax Global, your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1.Who we are

Showmax Global is a digital marketing and branding agency based at 16, Joshi Colony, Near Anand Hospital, Indore, Madhya Pradesh 452001, India. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at showmaxglobal@gmail.com.

2.Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to share with us, including:

  • Contact details you submit through our enquiry form or send us — such as your name, phone number, email address and project details.
  • Messages you send us via WhatsApp, email or social media.
  • Basic usage data your browser shares automatically (such as device type and pages visited) if we use website analytics tools.

3.How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • Provide the services you request.
  • Send you relevant updates about your project or our services (only where you'd expect us to).

We will never sell your personal information to anyone.

4.How your enquiry form works

When you submit our contact form, your details are formatted into a message that opens in WhatsApp on your device. You remain in control and choose whether to send it to us. We only receive your information once you send that message.

5.Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party services to operate our business, such as WhatsApp (Meta), email providers and website analytics. These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data. We share information with them only as needed to provide our services.

6.Cookies

Our website may use minimal cookies or similar technologies to function properly and, if analytics are enabled, to understand visitor activity. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

7.Data retention

We keep your information only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, deliver our services, and meet legal or business requirements. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

8.Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent for us to contact you. To exercise any of these rights, email us at showmaxglobal@gmail.com and we'll respond promptly.

9.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

10.Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at showmaxglobal@gmail.com or +91 70243 97999.

This privacy policy is a general template provided for your convenience and is not legal advice. As your business grows — especially before running paid ad campaigns — we recommend having it reviewed against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and any other regulations that apply to you.
